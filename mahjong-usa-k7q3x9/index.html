Project proposal

Mahjong USA

A simple events calendar for Mahjong players. A two-week starter project.

Prepared for Jennifer Keaton · Draft, November 2026 · Victoria Reed, victoriareed.ai

The problem

There is no one place to find Mahjong events

Questions all over social media

People keep asking where to play, shop and travel for Mahjong.

Events are scattered

Tournaments, cruises, social clubs and lessons live on many different sites.

Nothing sorts by date and place

No calendar lets a player filter by date, state and type of event.

Source: Mahjong USA ideas outline, August 16, 2026

What you get in two weeks

A simple calendar that fills itself

  • Find events by date, state and type
  • Events gathered automatically from as many sources as I can reliably reach, and checked before they go live
  • Plain pages that need no login
